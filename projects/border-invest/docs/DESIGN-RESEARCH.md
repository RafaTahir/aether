# Aether Design Research

Research reviewed on 2026-08-15. The interface is an original synthesis, not a replica of any one site.

## Investment Platforms

- [Kiva](https://www.kiva.org/): human project framing and approachable participation
- [Lendahand](https://www.lendahand.com/en-EU): project economics, funding progress, and visible risk language
- [OurCrowd](https://www.ourcrowd.com/): institutional trust and eligibility gating
- [Ethex](https://www.ethex.org.uk/): impact evidence beside investment terms
- [Goparity](https://goparity.com/): structured opportunity details and cross-border context
- [Bettervest](https://www.bettervest.com/en/): environmental project discovery
- [Abundance Investment](https://www.abundanceinvestment.com/): regulated warnings and opportunity status

## Consultancies

- [Bain](https://www.bain.com/): quantified outcomes and publication-style hierarchy
- [Dalberg](https://dalberg.com/): mission-led warmth with institutional credibility
- [IDEO](https://www.ideo.com/): outcome-led case storytelling
- [Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/): restrained editorial frame around project content
- [frog](https://www.frog.co/): strong point of view and transformation narrative
- [Wolff Olins](https://www.wolffolins.com/): project-first browsing
- [Lippincott](https://www.lippincott.com/): serif/sans editorial hierarchy and disciplined grids

## Applied Patterns

- One clear proposition rather than a rotating hero
- Human project context paired with comparable financial and impact metrics
- A flagship project followed by compact comparison rows rather than a uniform card wall
- Verification, operator type, milestones, and material risks exposed early
- Editorial serif for narrative moments, sans-serif for interfaces, mono for financial data
- Warm paper neutrals with one restrained amber accent
- Separate paths for participants and project operators
- Explicit devnet and fictional-data disclosures

## Deliberately Avoided

- Copying a competitor's brand, layout, language, or trade dress
- Generic violet crypto gradients and glass cards
- Artificial scarcity, countdowns, or manufactured urgency
- Promised returns or conflating revenue share with expected ROI
- Hidden risk and compliance language
- Dense trading-terminal conventions for a first-time consumer audience
