## Typography - Fraunces + Inter + IBM Plex Mono

- **Display:** Fraunces, reserved for narrative H1 and flagship project statements
- **Body and UI:** Inter
- **Numbers and addresses:** IBM Plex Mono with tabular numerals

Fonts are self-hosted at build time through `next/font/google` in `app/layout.tsx`.

## Gradients

- `--gradient-bg`: restrained champagne depth for hero and page-header sections
- `--gradient-accent`: bright gold-to-warm-amber accent for rules, markers, and non-text details

Use no more than one gradient in a viewport. Financial controls and body-copy backgrounds remain solid.

## Tone And Voice

Use direct, human, evidence-led language with quiet confidence. Name the project, location, operator, funding use, risk, and verification status before making broad claims. Prefer “Review the evidence” to “Unlock global opportunity.” Gold is a signal of importance, not proof of performance.

Avoid hype, artificial urgency, “revolutionary,” “seamless,” “exclusive,” and promised returns. Never confuse a revenue-share percentage with an expected investor return.

Voice example: “Solar irrigation for six farming cooperatives. Three releases, each tied to independently verified installation milestones.”

## Usage

- Use semantic color tokens rather than hardcoded colors.
- Use serif type sparingly; interfaces and dense content stay sans-serif.
- Use `font-mono tabular-nums` for all financial figures.
- Pair financial terms with impact evidence and material risks.
- Preserve generous whitespace and strong editorial hierarchy.
- Avoid glass effects, generic crypto gradients, dense trading-dashboard patterns, and identical card grids.
